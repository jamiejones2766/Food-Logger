One-time setup

This app sends what you type to Claude to work out the macros. It needs your own Anthropic API key. The key is stored on this device only and is never sent anywhere except Anthropic.

Get one at console.anthropic.com — you'll need a few pounds of credit on the account. Typical use here is well under £1 a month.
